Day 01 — My DevOps Learning Plan
My Current Level
I am an experienced Cloud and DevOps professional with hands-on experience in AWS, Linux, Terraform, Docker, Kubernetes, CI/CD, and cloud security. I am building on my existing knowledge through structured, practical tasks that strengthen my implementation, troubleshooting, automation, and production-readiness skills.
My 3 Goals
Build and document production-style projects: Create an end-to-end cloud-native application using AWS, Terraform, Docker, Kubernetes, and CI/CD.
Improve automation and reliability: Practice infrastructure as code, deployment automation, monitoring, troubleshooting, and recovery from common failures.
Strengthen cloud security and DevSecOps: Apply least-privilege IAM, secure configuration, vulnerability scanning, secrets management, and security checks in delivery pipelines.
My 3 Core Skills
AWS and Terraform: Design secure cloud infrastructure, build reusable modules, and understand state, drift, and recovery.
Docker, Kubernetes, and CI/CD: Containerize applications, deploy and troubleshoot workloads, and automate build, test, and release workflows.
Cloud Security and Observability: Apply security controls, investigate logs and metrics, and improve reliability through practical incident exercises.
My Learning Commitment
I will follow the TrainWithShubham #90DaysOfDevOps repository as a learning reference and organize my progress around practical tasks rather than a fixed calendar. I will move to the next task when I have completed the hands-on work and documented the result.
My Flexible Learning and Consistency Strategy
I will allocate learning time flexibly according to my availability and the complexity of each task, without setting a fixed daily schedule or deadline. I will focus on completing one meaningful task at a time, validating it with commands or tests, and recording the steps, results, and lessons learned in GitHub. When I am busy, I will resume from my last documented checkpoint rather than abandon the plan. I will review my progress regularly and prioritize practical implementation over passive watching.
My principle: Learn by doing, verify the result, document my work, and keep improving.
